# Developer Guide

Tools and workflows for developing MoreSailwindSails.

- [Adding boat support](Adding-boat-support) explains boat registration, stay
  geometry, optional additional control positions, mast ancestry, and
  contribution checks.
- [Building and contributing](Building-and-contributing) covers the development
  environment, plugin builds, automated checks, and contributions.
- [Profiling](Profiling) explains the Utils profiler for attributing CPU time
  and allocations to instrumented sail code, plus its frame and GC reports.
- [Benchmark](Benchmark) covers standalone whole-game frame captures and
  comparisons between native sails and MSS configurations.
- [Winch placement diagnostics](Winch-placement-diagnostics) covers mounting
  overlays, model previews, forced sheet fallbacks, and surface measurements.
- [mss command](MSS-command) documents the repository CLI, including asset
  baking, boat inspection, fallback halyard generation, installation and
  removal, benchmark commands, releases, decompilation, and log following.
